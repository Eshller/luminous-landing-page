Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for the enterprise data page of Adzzat Labs, an applied AI research lab. Create a cinematic 21:9 horizontal black-and-white analog photograph: an immense quiet archive room, long orderly rows of tall shelves holding paper folders and bound business records, receding to a central vanishing point. A single distant archivist is caught mid-stride with subtle long-exposure motion blur, seen from behind, small in the composition, no identifiable facial details. Sideways window light catches the edges of thousands of sheets; deep velvety blacks and warm ivory highlights, rich grain, restrained high contrast, beautiful actual paper textures, calm serious intellectual mood. Architecture and paper are the dominant subjects. Contemporary photographic realism, not illustration or 3D rendering. This must sit naturally beside grainy monochrome documentary photos and classic serif typography on a warm #f8f8f3 paper website with a near-black alternate theme. No UI, no diagrams, no glowing elements, no computers, no logos, no readable text, no watermarks. Image edge-to-edge, no border. Compose for a wide 21:9 crop with key details in the central horizontal band.

### public/enterprise-data/records.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial supporting photograph for an enterprise data partnership webpage by Adzzat Labs. A close, artful overhead oblique photograph of a person's hands carefully studying a thick open business ledger and sorting plain paper records on a worn dark wooden desk. The left hand gently holds an edge of a page, the right hand holds a simple pencil. Sheets with tiny indistinct abstract marks, nothing legible, no numbers or personal information. Window light cuts diagonally across paper and fingertips, deep soft shadows. Analogue black-and-white documentary photography, warm ivory highlights, silver-gelatin film grain, tactile realism, restrained intellectual atmosphere. Composition is 4:5 portrait, crop excludes face, focus on hands and physical paper as a metaphor for human judgment within enterprise records. No logos, no captions, no UI, no bright colors, no screens, no artificial glow, no watermark. Fits a premium minimalist website with classic Newsreader serif type and warm ivory / near black themes.
